# Porch Cats — research brief (Sep 6 2026)

Ask: one-shot a cat game (or EDM / Kalshi-style gambling). Copy a winner, add a small twist from review feedback, monetize exactly the way the competitors do.

## Why cat game, not the other two
- Real-money prediction markets (Kalshi) need a CFTC-registered exchange or state gambling licences plus App Review 5.3 documentation; not one-shot-able. A play-money version is "simulated gambling", 17+, and a different product.
- EDM (Groovepad-style pad launchers) are sample-pack businesses; quality is the audio content, which cannot be vibe-coded to a competitive standard.
- Cat games are the biggest cozy category on the store and the collector sub-genre is code-drawable.

**Pick: Neko Atsume.** The loop is small, fully time-based, and the art is stylised enough that code-drawn SVG cats can carry it. Its monetization is the cleanest in the genre (no ads; consumable premium currency) and its sequel added the subscription layer, which is what we want for LTV.

## Review feedback (most-recent reviews, Sep 2026)
Neko Atsume / Neko Atsume 2:
- "not always cats when u check" — cats arrive too rarely, especially early (5 mentions across both).
- Want to **pet / interact** with the cats (2) and want **outfits** (2).
- **Gold fish scarcity**, can't convert silver → gold in NA2 (5). "Gold fish seem rare."
- Toys constantly needing repair depletes fish (2).
- Want a **notification when cats arrive** (1); want more yard space (3); want more cats (2).
- Loved: cute art, relaxing, no ads, checking in "at work away from my cat".
Adorable Home: ads are the #1 complaint by far (15+), "love" is hard to earn (5), nothing to do (7). → **no ads**, generous currency.

## The twist (small, review-driven)
1. **Petting.** Tap a cat on the porch: purr haptic, hearts, +bond. Bond raises gift size, unlocks a memento at bond 5 and a portrait in the Catbook. (The #1 interaction ask.)
2. **Traces.** Cats you missed leave a note: "Biscuit loafed in the box for 2h, left 14 silver". The porch is never empty of evidence. (Answers "no cats when I check".)
3. **Fish exchange.** 500 silver → 10 gold once a day, free. (The #1 NA2 complaint.) Club doubles it.
4. **Arrival alerts.** Local notification when a bowl runs empty and when a rare cat is due.
No toy wear. No ads. Ever.

## Monetization (mirrors the competitors exactly)
- Gold fish packs (consumable): 50 / $0.99 · 120 / $1.99 · 200 / $2.99 · 300 / $3.99 — Neko Atsume's ladder.
- **Cat Club** subscription: $3.99/mo · $24.99/yr, 7-day free trial — bowls never empty (helper cat refills), 5 gold fish a day, 2× fish exchange, Backyard unlocked, Club-only cat (Jeeves). This is Neko Atsume 2's Cat's Club plus Cat Game's VIP framing, priced between them.
- Backyard expansion 180 gold (or Club). Rare food and premium items priced in gold; the rest in silver.
- No ads, no energy, no timers on purchases.
